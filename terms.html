Payment

One adult pays for the whole home: ₹99 a month or ₹999 a year. Family members use the home under that subscription. The cook never pays. Payments are not refunded. Stopping later does not return money already paid.

Roles

The paying adult manages the home and confirms check-ins when location is weak. Family members set the menu, grocery, and leftovers, and can invite the cook. Invites work by a code and last 48 hours.

Location

Check-in shows near or far for that home only. There is no continuous tracking and no exact pin shown to the family.

Respect

Use the app to share the day’s work kindly. Do not use it to harass anyone.

Messages

We send alerts about the home and the day’s work only. No marketing messages.

Leaving

You can stop at any time. To delete your account, use Settings → Your account in the app, or write to connect@darntech.games. Deletion waits 45 days, and you can cancel until that day. Read Delete your account.
