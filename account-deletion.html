Delete your account

You can delete your Ghar Sorted account from the app: Settings, then Your account.

  • We wait 45 days. You can cancel any day until then, and the account stays open.
  • After 45 days your login is removed and you leave every home.
  • If you were the only person in a home, that home is removed too.
  • If you are the paying adult and other people are still in the home, the account cannot close until that home no longer has other people.
  • Payments are not refunded.

The paying adult can also reset a home from the same screen. That clears the menu, grocery, leftovers, check-ins, leave, saved dishes, and invite codes. The people in the home stay.
